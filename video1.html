Video 1 | HTML Elements and Attributes

The video introduces HTML elements and attributes, explaining how they are used to build and organize content on a webpage. It shows how elements use opening and closing tags, while attributes provide additional information or settings for those elements.

The lesson also helps beginners understand how HTML tags and attributes work together to create structured webpages.
