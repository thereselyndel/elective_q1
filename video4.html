Video 4 | Titles & Paragraphs in HTML

The video explains how to use headings and paragraphs to organize and present text on a webpage. It introduces heading elements such as h1 to h6, which are used for titles and subtitles, and the p element for paragraphs.

It also explains how using these elements properly helps make webpage content more structured and easier to read.
